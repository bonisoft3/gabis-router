GBRouter

Bike routing on your terms.

Why GBRouter exists

GBRouter is built by cycling nerds, for cycling nerds. It is made for people who care about how a route feels. Most route planners treat you like someone who just wants to get from A to B. They hide the map data and make decisions for you.

BRouter is a great alternative. It is a configurable routing engine that scores every road based on custom cycling profiles. But the classic web interface at brouter-web is quite limited, and tweaking routing behaviour means writing a custom profile DSL.

GBRouter brings the BRouter engine directly into your browser using WebAssembly. There is no separate server to run. It also puts the nerdy details at the centre: surfaces, road types, traffic, speed limits, and noise estimates. It is designed for cyclists who want to tweak their route until it feels right.

Offline and private

The router runs entirely in your browser using WebAssembly with no accounts, no tracking, and no server queries. Download the routing data and map files once, and the whole thing works in airplane mode or out in the hills with zero phone reception.

GBRouter is open source and free forever.

Open source credits

GBRouter builds on great work by the open source mapping and cycling community:

  • OpenStreetMap for map data, road classifications, and surface tags
  • BRouter for the original routing engine and profile algorithms by Arndt Brenschede
  • MapLibre GL for map rendering
  • Protomaps and PMTiles for offline vector map tiles
  • Photon by Komoot for search and geocoding
